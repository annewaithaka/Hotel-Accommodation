A note from the owners

We run three houses, and we know every guest by the time they leave.

Baraka Collection began with one small guesthouse in Diani, and grew, quietly, to two more. We kept them intentionally small so that the person who checks you in is also the one who remembers how you take your coffee on the second morning.

The properties

Choose your Kenya.

Coast, lake, or city. Each house has its own character — but the same welcome runs through all three.

The coast

Baraka Diani.

Ukunda, Kwale County · beachfront

Twelve rooms opening onto the Indian Ocean. Palms, seagrass ceilings, and the kind of long, empty beach walks that make an afternoon disappear. Best for honeymooners, families, and anyone who needs to hear waves for a week.

See Diani
The lake

Baraka Naivasha.

Lake Naivasha, Nakuru County · lakeside lodge

Eight lodge suites set among acacia and fever trees, twenty minutes from Hell's Gate. Watch fish eagles at breakfast, drift out on the lake in the afternoon. Quiet in a way the city forgets exists.

See Naivasha
The city

Baraka Nairobi.

Riverside, Nairobi · a small city residence

A restored 1970s residence tucked into leafy Riverside — fourteen rooms, a courtyard, a good kitchen, and everything you need for a working week in Nairobi that still feels human. Ten minutes from the CBD.

What runs through all three

Small. Careful. Kenyan.

The details we don't compromise on, wherever you stay with us.

Under fifteen rooms.

Every property is kept small on purpose. You will not queue for breakfast, and the staff will know your name by the second day.

Cooked from where we are.

Fish landed that morning in Diani. Tilapia from the lake in Naivasha. Nyama choma from a butcher two streets over in Nairobi.

Local, first.

Our teams are hired from the towns we operate in. Our guides are Kenyan. So is the coffee, the tea, the linen, and most of the furniture.
