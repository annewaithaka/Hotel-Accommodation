About us

One family. Three houses.

The story is simple. We opened one small hotel in Diani, guests kept asking us to open another, and eventually we did — twice.

The story

We didn't set out to build a group.

Baraka started in 2012 as a twelve-room guesthouse in Diani, run by two people and a very small team. For a long time, that's all it was, and we were happy with it.

Guests kept asking us the same thing on the way to the airport: where would you go if we wanted more of this, somewhere else in Kenya? We didn't have a good answer.

So in 2019 we opened Baraka Naivasha, a small lodge by the lake. Nairobi followed in 2023 — a family residence a friend of ours had been renting out, restored quietly over two years.

All three are still small. All three are staffed almost entirely by people from the towns they sit in. And all three are run the way the first one was: by owners who are there, and staff who care.

How we work

The things we don't compromise on.

Small on purpose.

No property will ever have more than fifteen rooms. That's the ceiling. It's how we keep the welcome real.

Hired locally.

Our teams live in the towns we operate in. Our guides are Kenyan. Our suppliers are the ones down the road.

Owned, not managed.

You're staying in a family business, not a franchise. If something isn't right, tell us. We'll fix it before the day is out.
